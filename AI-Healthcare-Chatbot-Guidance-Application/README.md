# 📚 AI Healthcare Chatbot Research

This repository contains research and review papers related to an **AI-powered Healthcare Chatbot** developed to provide accessible, conversational, and personalized healthcare guidance using **Artificial Intelligence (AI)**, **Natural Language Processing (NLP)**, and **Machine Learning**.

---

## 🎯 Research Objective

The research explores the use of **AI-based chatbot technology** to provide users with accessible and conversational healthcare information.

The proposed system focuses on:

* Understanding user healthcare queries
* Analyzing reported symptoms
* Providing possible disease-related information
* Offering healthcare guidance and care suggestions
* Supporting personalized recommendations
* Improving accessibility to healthcare information through conversational AI
